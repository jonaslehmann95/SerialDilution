%
Pipetting reserve: every tube is made with this much extra on top of what it has to give out (your volume and the volume passed on), so you do not run dry along the series.

Scheme

Protocol

Everything runs in your browser; inputs are saved locally. In serial mode tubes are prepared from the highest to the lowest concentration; each tube is made large enough to supply the next one.
